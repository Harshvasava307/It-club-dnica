How does the Leadership & Hierarchy works in It Club?

Since Foundation, The Head / President of It Club is elected by Former Commitee of IT club's Management Circle. The President/Head of It Club is responsible and Key role for managing Tech and other Events, Creating a new Circle of It Club every year. handeling Resources such like Registration Portal, Official Website of It Club Records etc.. All Members and Voluenteers of Club are given equal rights to vote and present suggestion regarding any event or Tech Activity. We believe in Learning and Teaching.

How to Join It Club?

The most Unique thing about It Club is that there are no rules or Protocols to Join It Club. You can join It Club simply by Contacting any Voluenteering or Key member of It Club. After Joining you will be given daily tasks(Progaming definitons, Interview Questions, Digtal Illustrarion) to improve your Skill set as a Complete Programmer.
